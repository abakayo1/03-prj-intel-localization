Milestones

Key moments in Intel's sustainability journey

1968

Intel Founded

Built on innovation Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, creating the foundation for decades of innovation in computing and responsible technology development.

Built on innovation

1971

First Microprocessor

Small chip, big impact Intel introduces the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, beginning a new era of efficient computing that would power innovative devices across the globe.

Small chip, big impact

1978

8086 Processor

Architecture for growth The 8086 creates a lasting architecture standard that supports performance gains, energy-efficient design, and the scalable computing ecosystem we rely on today.

Architecture for growth

1985

386 Processor

More power, smarter computing Intel launches the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, bringing powerful multitasking capabilities to personal computers and setting a new standard for computing efficiency.

More power, smarter computing

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Turning point for action Intel reaches its highest annual greenhouse gas emissions, a turning point that drives major investments in chemical abatement, renewable power, and energy-efficient manufacturing.

Turning point for action

2020

RISE Strategy

Purpose-driven goals Intel unveils its RISE strategy and 2030 goals, focusing on climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction while driving inclusive, sustainable innovation across the industry.

Purpose-driven goals

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Zero-carbon ambition Intel commits to net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across its global operations by 2040, reinforcing a long-term approach to environmental accountability and clean energy adoption.

Zero-carbon ambition

2023

Renewable Electricity

Cleaner power at scale Intel reaches 99% renewable electricity worldwide, reducing its carbon footprint and helping accelerate the transition to more sustainable manufacturing and data center operations.

Cleaner power at scale

2024

Sustainability Summit

Collaboration unlocks progress Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit to bring together suppliers, government leaders, and industry partners around the future of next-generation sustainable semiconductor production.

Collaboration unlocks progress
